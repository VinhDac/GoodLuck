Installing

  • Windows protected your PC: press More info, then Run anyway. It may ask again for each new version.
  • macOS cannot check GoodLuck: close the message, then System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
  • No Run anyway or Open Anyway, or the file is blocked: the computer belongs to an organisation that blocks unknown programs. Install GoodLuck! on a computer of your own.
  • The installer says GoodLuck! is in use: let it close GoodLuck! for you, as it offers, or quit GoodLuck! first.

Opening the app

  • GoodLuck! is the small bar at the top of your screen, with its chat under it. Its minus button puts it away; the duck by the clock brings it back.
  • While a session runs, the bar rests as a duck in the top-left corner. Press it to open the bar again.
  • To quit, right-click the duck by the clock: Quit GoodLuck!. On a Mac the duck is in the menu bar.
  • Only one copy runs at a time. Another copy of GoodLuck! is already running: quit that copy first, from its duck.
  • A window with an error and nothing else: GoodLuck! could not start. Report it, with the text of the error.

Setting up

  • That PDF has no text in it: it is a scan. Export the CV again from Word or Google Docs, as a PDF or a .docx.
  • That PDF cannot be opened: it asks for a password: save a copy without the password, or save the CV as a .docx.
  • Only a Word file (.docx) or a PDF can be imported: save the CV as one of the two.
  • Set up first stays on the bar: the chat lists what is still missing, and opens the first of it. Your profile, the job mailbox and your target are needed; your phone and the assistant are not.
  • The lists of job titles and skills stay empty: the first time, GoodLuck! reads real postings to build them, which takes about half a minute and needs the internet.
  • A new computer: on the old one, Settings, Export / Import, Export everything; on the new one, Import a file… in the setup chat.

The job mailbox

  • this mailbox (…) differs from the profile's address: the mailbox has to be your contact email, the one employers reply to. Connect that address, or change it in your profile.
  • that is not an app password (16 lowercase letters): paste the 16 letters Google showed, not your Google password.
  • Gmail refused: the app password is wrong, or was deleted, or your Google password changed since. Make a new one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  • could not reach: no internet, or a firewall in the way. Try again on another network.
  • Google has no App passwords page for you: turn on 2-Step Verification first. A work or school account may not allow app passwords at all: use a personal account.

Your phone

  • That is not a token.: copy the whole line BotFather gave you, the digits before the colon included.
  • The token works, … but nobody has messaged it yet.: open the link it gives, press Start in your bot, then press Save again.
  • Telegram rejected the token: get a new token from BotFather, paste it and press Save.
  • The bot does not answer: GoodLuck! has to be running on your computer.
  • No message at all: Settings, Notifications: is All messages on, and the kind you expect? Press Test.

The assistant

  • It is optional: without it, the chat finds the app's screens and actions by the letters you type.
  • that is not a Groq key (one starts with gsk_): copy the key from its page again, the whole of it.
  • Groq does not know this key: copy it again from its page, or make a new one.
  • Groq could not be reached: check the connection, then paste the key again.
  • The free key has a limit each day. The small ring by the chat's line shows how much of today's is left.
  • A smarter one, at a cost: Settings, General, Assistant model, Claude (API key). Press Get a key, make one in your Claude Console, paste it. Its answers are billed to that account by use; choose its model and how long it thinks. When it cannot answer, Groq does, if its key is kept.
  • that is not a Claude API key (one starts with sk-ant-): copy the key from its page again, the whole of it.
  • Anthropic does not know this key: copy it again from its page, or make a new one.
  • Claude refused the request: Your credit balance is too low…: add credit in your Claude Console, under Billing.

Sessions

  • Nothing happens every hour: is the session on? The duck by the clock says On watch · next session in … min when it is. A computer that sleeps runs nothing.
  • … is running: wait for it to finish, or press Stop: one round at a time.
  • could not launch Chrome: install Google Chrome, or check that Microsoft Edge is there.
  • The job sites wait at night: the rounds that run by themselves read them only between the hours in Settings, Run (8:00 to 22:00 unless you change them). Run a round now reads them at any hour.
  • A message A stage failed on your phone: open the journal for why.

Send CV

  • It fills each form in a browser window of its own, the Apply window, and leaves its Submit to you. When the page says it was received, it goes on to the next.
  • no Submit in 15 min; the round stops here: the form waited for your Submit. Send CV starts with it again next time.
  • A question it cannot answer: it asks you once, on your phone and in the chat. Your answer is kept for every form that asks it.
  • Your turn: the page asks what only a person can do. Do it in the Apply window, then press Done.
  • A site wants you signed in: press Open its form, sign in there, then Fill it in now.
  • A job it does not open (it asks for more years than you have, or it is in a country you did not choose): it is in the Queue, under By hand, or skip, with why.

Jobs and scores

  • Too few jobs: add job titles to your profile, following the suggestions. Then look at Dropped in Search: each job says why it was set aside. Keep it brings one in.
  • No score: Not read yet means its page has not been read: the next round reads it.
  • Scores look low: open a job's Why this score, and the CV screen. Add to your CV the skills you have and it does not mention.
  • A job you applied to is still listed: press I applied on its card or its page.

Your data

  • Everything is on your computer, in one folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\jobbot on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/jobbot on a Mac. See Privacy.
  • That is not a GoodLuck! file: choose the .tar.gz file Export made.
  • That file was made by a newer GoodLuck!: install the newest version, then import it.

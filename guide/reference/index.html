Every screen

Each tab of GoodLuck!, and what each of its buttons does. The screens are the real app with fictional data: press anything in them.

On this page: Home · Search · A job · Track · Queue · CV · Profile · Settings · Phone · Your data

Home

  • The bar: LIVE while a session is on, then today's numbers: new jobs, applications sent, taken further, rejections.
  • Start session starts one. With a session on it reads Run a round now, and Running… while a round runs. Stop ends the session.
  • Queue opens the mail waiting for you, with how many. ⚟ (three sliders) chooses what a session contains, and the sources it reads.
  • Best score: the jobs at or above your mark, newest found first.
  • Last 7 days: new jobs and applications sent, per day. Its four-corners button shows 30 days and a table of every day, with a total.
  • Applications: where they all stand, Sent today, and Replies · last 14 days.
  • The journal: what the app is doing, newest line first. The line at the very bottom of the window says whether the session is on, and when the next round is due.

Search

  • The bar: how many jobs are kept, and how many are best scores. Run searches once now, without reading mail. Clear store deletes the postings to search again from scratch (it asks twice); jobs you applied to are kept.
  • The search box finds a title or a company.
  • Kept, Dropped, All: what matched your profile, what was set aside, or everything. A dropped job says why (title does not match any target title, for one), and Keep it brings it in.
  • Sort by: Score, Newest, Oldest, Company, Title.
  • Score: a slider with two ends, for a range of scores. Applied and Expired show the jobs kept out of the list; Not read yet shows only the jobs without a score yet.
  • Place: Anywhere, Near me, one of your countries, Remote. Then who posted it (Direct employer or Agencies) and where it came from. Each chip says how many.
  • ⚟ on the bar: the sources, the job titles you aim at, the levels you accept and the countries you want, with Apply. It is your profile: a change here re-scores the jobs.

A job's page

  • The title, the company, the place, and the score.
  • Open the original posting: the link to the posting on the employer's or the job site's page.
  • Why this score: every thing the posting asks for, how often, and the line of your CV that shows it, or nothing on your profile says it.
  • The posting: its full text.
  • Track: I applied puts it on Track. Once it is there, it says since when and where it stands, with Open in Track and Undo.
  • Still open?: Expired takes it out of Search, Home and the CV tab, with Undo. A posting its employer stopped listing is marked expired by itself.

Track

  • The bar: applied, taken further, rejected, and waiting on you. Scan mail reads the mailbox now, Queue opens the mail to decide, Clear table deletes the applications rebuilt from mail (it asks twice; the ones you applied to through the app stay).
  • ⚟ on the bar: Treat as rejected after 10, 14, 20, 30 or 45 days of silence. 20 unless you change it.
  • The table: search by company or role, and narrow it by source, by who applied, and by situation.
  • The groups: They are talking to you, Still inside the reply window, Treated as rejected (your silence mark, not a mail) and Settled.
  • Open a row: every mail about it, each with Read the full mail, the link to the posting, and change to to set its state by hand: interview, offer, rejected, back to waiting.

The Queue

  • Moves it proposes: a mail that would move an application. Accept moves it; Skip leaves it. A mail whose application is not on the table asks Whose is it?: type a firm or a job title, or pick a guess.
  • Mail it could not read, about an application on the table: say what it is (acknowledgement, interview invitation, rejection, offer), or skip.
  • Which posting: an application the app could not tie to a posting. that is the one ties it; applied elsewhere marks it as applied outside the app.
  • Empty, it says Nothing is waiting on you.

CV

  • Missing from your CV: what the jobs in Search ask for that your CV never says; how many jobs ask, and +N, how many more would reach your mark if it did. Open a row for the employers' own words. ✕ stops a word that is not a skill from counting anywhere; Not counted and ↺ bring it back.
  • Your CV: Look shows it page by page, Save a copy puts it in Downloads, ✕ removes it. + Add a CV (.docx or PDF) adds another: each job is then read with the CV that suits it best.
  • Save report: the findings in your Downloads folder, as a PDF or as Markdown (.md) for an AI assistant.
  • By role and What the jobs ask for: the same findings by kind of job, and as a cloud of words.

Profile

  • Every answer you gave, in three parts, each with edit. Ready to search when the required ones are in.
  • Import a CV (.docx or PDF) reads a CV again and offers what it finds for the answers still empty.
  • Every save keeps a new version of the profile, and re-scores the jobs.
  • The parts, their questions and their suggestion lists are in step 3.

Settings

TabWhat is there
GeneralTheme (Dark, Light, System), Colour (Green, Blue, Purple, Orange, Pink, Steel, Rainbow, or your own), When the app opens (wait for me, or start a session), and Score mark (50+ to 90+, each with how many jobs reach it). Changes apply at once.
NotificationsYour Telegram bot: Bot token, Save, Test; Messages to your phone, each kind on or off; the hour of the end-of-day report. See step 5.
RunRescan every how many minutes (5 to 1440), the hours the browser may work in (job sites are read only then), and the pace of reading job sites: Gentle, Normal or Fast.
SourcesThe company boards a search reads (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) and job-alert mail, each with how many jobs it brought in.
GmailThe connected mailbox, Change the app password, Watch the mailbox (off, or every 1, 5 or 15 minutes), and Look back: how many days of mail each pass reads again (30 unless you change it).
StatusHow each part of the app is doing: the browser, the boards being read, what waits to be scored again.
Export / ImportExport everything writes one file into Downloads; Import a file… takes one back. See Your data.
Start overBack to a fresh start: type DELETE, then Delete everything, start over. A backup goes to your Desktop first.

Your data

  • Where it is: everything GoodLuck! keeps is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\jobbot on your computer. The program itself is apart, so installing a new version keeps it all.
  • Passwords: the Gmail app password and the Telegram token are in Windows Credential Manager, not in that folder.
  • Moving to another computer: Settings, Export / Import, Export everything writes GoodLuck-export-…tar.gz into Downloads. On the new computer, Import a file…, on Setting up or in Settings, takes it all back, connections included. The file holds your passwords: keep it to yourself, and delete it once it is imported.
  • Removing GoodLuck!: Settings, Start over clears everything, the passwords in Credential Manager included, after leaving a backup on your Desktop (it holds your passwords too: delete it if you do not need it). Then uninstall GoodLuck in Windows Settings, Apps, and delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\jobbot.

Step 5 of 7 · about 3 minutes

Connect your phone

With Telegram, GoodLuck! reaches you wherever you are: the new jobs after each search, every application that moves, a report each evening. And from your phone you can ask it for jobs, run a round, or have your CV sent to you.

You needTelegram on your phone, signed in. It is free on the App Store and Google Play. GoodLuck! talks to you through a bot of your own, which you make in two minutes with Telegram's @BotFather.

1. Make your bot

  1. In Telegram, search for @BotFather. Take the one with the blue tick, and press Start.
  2. Send /newbot.
  3. It asks for a name: anything you like, such as My GoodLuck.
  4. It asks for a username, which has to end in bot and not be taken yet, such as rowan_goodluck_bot.
  5. It answers with a token: a long line like 7123456789:AAH…. Copy the whole line, digits and colon included. Keep it to yourself: whoever has it can speak as your bot.

A drawing of the chat with BotFather, kept to what matters. Your token is longer.

2. Give the token to GoodLuck!

  1. Open Settings, with the cog at the foot of the sidebar, then the tab Notifications. It reads Not connected., above the same four steps.
  2. Paste the token in Bot token and press Save. GoodLuck! finds your bot and asks you to message it first, with a link: Open t.me/…, press Start, then press Save again.
  3. Open that link: Telegram opens your bot. Press Start.
  4. Back in GoodLuck!, press Save again. The box can stay empty: this time GoodLuck! finds your chat, remembers it, and says ✅ That worked.

Still on the Setting up screen? Its card Your phone does the same, with its own Connect button.

3. Test it

  1. Still in Settings, Notifications, it now reads ✓ Connected.
  2. Press Test. This arrives on your phone:
Done whenthe test message is on your phone.

What it sends you

Four kinds of message. Each is switched on or off in Settings, Notifications, under Messages to your phone, and All messages holds them all at once.

New jobs after each search

After a search that brought in new jobs: up to ten, best score first, each title a link to the posting. A search with nothing new sends nothing.

Mail updates

After each pass through your mailbox: the applications that moved, an interview or an offer first, and the mail now waiting for you in the Queue.

End-of-day report

Once a day, at 20:00 unless you change the hour in Settings, Notifications: today's numbers, the best new jobs you have not applied to, and what is waiting for you. If GoodLuck! is closed at that hour, the report goes out when you open it later that evening.

A stage failed

When a round could not run, so that GoodLuck! never sits idle for days without you knowing. It is said once a day.

At night, outside the hours set in Settings, Run (8:00 to 22:00 unless you change them), these four arrive without a sound. Answers to your commands always ring.

What you can ask it

Send a command in the chat with your bot. /help lists them:

Find jobs: /jobs

/jobs takes the filters of the Search tab, in any order and any mix:

WriteTo get
wordsjobs whose title or company contains them, such as /jobs data analyst
70+ or 60-80a score of at least 70, or between 60 and 80
7d, 30d or 90dposted in the last 7, 30 or 90 days (a posting with no date is left out)
remote or nearremote jobs only, or jobs near where you are based
a countryjobs there, written as one word: uk, usa, singapore
direct or agencyjobs from direct employers only, or from agencies only
new or oldnewest or oldest first (otherwise best score first)
applied or expiredalso the jobs you applied to, or the expired ones

The jobs come ten at a time, with Next 10 ▸ under them. Each one carries its number as /job_…: tap it to open that job.

One job: /job

The job, what it asks for that your CV does not say, and three buttons: Send CV for this job, I applied, which puts it on Track, and Expired.

The other commands

CommandWhat it does
/applicationsYour applications and where they stand, newest first.
/mailThe mail waiting for your decision, with Accept and Skip buttons.
/statusToday's numbers, and whether the session is on.
/runRuns a round now, and keeps the session on.
/stopStops the session.
/cvSends you your CV, after asking: Send or Cancel.
/reportSends you the CV report, as a PDF and a .md file, after asking.

You can also send a CV (.docx or PDF) to the chat: the bot offers to add it, or to replace one you have.

Good to know

  • Only your own chat is answered. Anyone else who finds your bot gets no reply.
  • GoodLuck! has to be open on your computer for the bot to answer and to send. Commands sent while it is closed are dropped, not run later.
  • The token is kept in Windows Credential Manager. A CV goes to Telegram only after you press Send, and Telegram keeps what is sent there.
If it does not work
  • That is not a token.: copy the whole line from BotFather, the digits before the colon included.
  • The token works, … but nobody has messaged it yet.: open the link, press Start in your bot, then Save again.
  • Could not send: wrong or revoked token: in BotFather, send /token for a new token, or make a new bot. Paste it and press Save.
  • Nothing arrives: is GoodLuck! open, and is All messages on in Settings, Notifications?

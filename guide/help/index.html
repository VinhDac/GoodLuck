When something is wrong

Find what you see, then what to do. The journal, along the bottom of Home, Search and Track, says what the app did and what it could not do: start there.

On this page: Installing · Opening · Profile and CV · Gmail · Telegram · Sessions · Jobs and scores · Your data

Installing

  • Windows protected your PC: press More info, then Run anyway. It may ask again for each new version.
  • macOS cannot check GoodLuck, or says Apple could not verify it: close the message, then System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. It may ask again for each new version.
  • No Run anyway or Open Anyway, or the file is blocked: the computer belongs to an organisation that blocks unknown programs. Install GoodLuck! on a computer of your own.
  • The installer says GoodLuck! is in use: let it close GoodLuck! for you, as it offers, or quit GoodLuck! first. On a Mac, the old GoodLuck cannot be replaced while it is open: quit it first.

Opening the app

  • Only one copy of GoodLuck! runs at a time. Opening it again brings back the window of the one already running.
  • Another copy of GoodLuck! is already running: quit that copy first, from its key by the clock, Quit GoodLuck!. On a Mac, quit it from its icon in the Dock.
  • The window is closed but the key is still by the clock: a session is on and still working. Click the key to bring the window back, or right-click it, Quit GoodLuck!. On a Mac the key is in the menu bar: click it for Open GoodLuck! or Quit GoodLuck!.
  • A window with an error and nothing else: GoodLuck! could not start. Report it, with the text of the error.

Profile and CV

  • That PDF has no text in it: it is a scan. Export the CV again from Word or Google Docs, as a PDF or a .docx.
  • That PDF cannot be opened: it asks for a password: save a copy without the password, or save the CV as a .docx.
  • Only a Word file (.docx) or a PDF can be imported: save the CV as one of the two.
  • File too large (limit 8MB): save the CV without its pictures.
  • The Setting up screen will not go away: it needs every required answer (six in Who you are, all of What you want, the years in Work experience) and the job mailbox connected. Each part shows how many of its answers are in.
  • The suggestion lists stay empty: the first time, GoodLuck! reads real postings to build them, which takes about half a minute and needs the internet.

Gmail

  • this mailbox (…) differs from the profile's address: the mailbox has to be your Contact email. Connect that address, or change Contact email in Profile, Who you are.
  • that is not an app password (16 lowercase letters): paste the 16 letters Google showed, not your Google password.
  • there is no app password yet: paste it in the box under the address.
  • Gmail refused.: the app password is wrong, or was deleted, or your Google password changed since. Make a new one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  • could not reach: no internet, or a firewall in the way. Try again on another network.
  • Google has no App passwords page for you: turn on 2-Step Verification first. A work or school account may not allow app passwords at all: use a personal Gmail account.
  • To check the mailbox still works, press Scan mail on Track and read the journal.

Telegram

  • That is not a token.: copy the whole line BotFather gave you, the digits before the colon included.
  • The token works, … but nobody has messaged it yet.: open the link it gives, press Start in your bot, then press Save again.
  • Telegram rejected the token or wrong or revoked token: get a new token from BotFather (/token), paste it and press Save.
  • wrong chat id, or you have not messaged the bot yet: send your bot a message, then press Save.
  • Changed to a new bot: press Start in the new bot, press Save, then quit and reopen GoodLuck! so it listens to the new one.
  • The bot does not answer: GoodLuck! has to be open on your computer. Messages sent while it was closed are dropped.
  • No message at all: Settings, Notifications: is All messages on, and the kind you expect? Press Test.

Sessions

  • … is running: wait for it to finish, or press Stop: one round at a time. Wait for it, or press Stop.
  • could not launch Chrome: install Google Chrome, or check that Microsoft Edge is there.
  • The job sites wait at night: the rounds that run by themselves read them only between the hours in Settings, Run (8:00 to 22:00 unless you change them). Run a round now reads them at any hour.
  • job-alert mail: no mailbox connected: connect the job mailbox (step 4).
  • A message A stage failed on your phone: open the app and read the journal for why.
  • Nothing happens every hour: is the session on? The bottom line says on watch · next round in … min when it is. A computer that sleeps runs nothing.

Jobs and scores

  • Too few jobs: add job titles (Profile, What you want), following the suggestions. Then look at Dropped on Search: each job says why it was set aside, such as title does not match any target title, a senior title, a country you did not choose, or no visa sponsor. Keep it brings one in. Check too that every source is on (⚟ on Search).
  • No score: Not read yet means its page has not been read: the next round reads it. A job that asks for too little to measure keeps no score: read it yourself.
  • Scores look low: open a job's Why this score, and the CV tab. Add to your CV the skills you have and it does not mention, or add them to Your skills.
  • A job you applied to is still listed: press I applied on its page, and it leaves the list.

Your data

  • Moving to another computer: Settings, Export / Import, Export everything; on the new one, Import a file….
  • That is not a GoodLuck! file: choose the .tar.gz file Export made.
  • That file was made by a newer GoodLuck!: install the newest version, then import it.
  • Removing everything: see Your data.

Still stuck?

Report a problem: say what you did, what you saw, and the lines of the journal around it. Never post a password, a token or your CV there.

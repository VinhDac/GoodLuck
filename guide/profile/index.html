Step 3 of 7 · about 5 minutes

Your profile

GoodLuck! opens on Setting up: three parts about you, then two connections. The screen stays in front of the app until the required answers are in and the job mailbox is connected, in step 4.

Start from your CV

  1. Press Import your CV (.docx or PDF) and choose your CV. The button shows Reading and the file's name while it works.
  2. A page called Approve what goes in lists what it found: your name, email, phone and town, your education, the skills it recognised, the titles of the jobs you have held. Only the answers still empty are offered, and every row is ticked.
  3. Untick what you do not want, then press Save selected. GoodLuck! takes you to the first part that still misses an answer.

No CV at hand? Press or type it yourself instead.

Who you are

Six answers here are required: Full name, Contact email, Phone, Where you're based, Nationality (as on your passport) and Education, one degree per row with + add a degree.

  • Contact email has to be the Gmail address you connect in step 4: the one employers reply to.
  • Where you're based decides which jobs count as near you.
  • Your education and certifications count towards your scores.

Profile links, notice period, salary and certifications are optional.

What you want: the part that matters most

Your job titles decide which postings are kept: a posting whose title contains none of them is set aside. The wording employers use matters, so Quant Researcher and Quantitative Researcher are two different titles to a job site. The more wordings you add, the more jobs you see.

  1. Which job titles would you take? Click the suggestions under the box. They are real titles from real postings, and they follow what you pick: choose Quantitative Researcher and its family comes up, from Quant Researcher to Quantitative Research Analyst. Keep clicking: 15 to 20 titles is a good start.
  2. To find one, type a few letters of each word, in any order: quant res finds Quantitative Researcher. Nothing fits? Press Enter and the title is added exactly as you typed it.
  3. Your skills: the same, with the skills postings ask for. They follow your picks too: choose pandas and the skills asked for alongside it come up. Add what you could talk about in an interview.
  4. Levels you'd accept, from Intern / Placement to Lead / Staff / Principal. If you tick any of the first four (Intern, Graduate, Graduate scheme, Junior), a posting whose title says it is senior is set aside, even if you tick the higher levels too.
  5. Where do you want to work? Countries, one by one. Add Anywhere (remote) to keep remote jobs from any country too.
  6. Where can you work without a visa sponsor? In any other country, a posting that says it does not sponsor visas is set aside. Need a sponsor everywhere? Choose Nowhere (I need a sponsor).
  7. Working arrangement: Fully remote, Hybrid, On-site. Tick On-site and nothing is set aside for its arrangement; tick only Fully remote and a posting not marked remote is set aside.
The very first timethe suggestion lists stay empty for about half a minute, while GoodLuck! reads real postings to build them. Carry on with the other questions: the lists appear when they are ready, and nothing you filled in is lost.

Work experience

How many years have you ACTUALLY worked? is required: give the real number. Your jobs and projects under it are optional, one row each with + add a job and + add a project, and one sentence per line for what you did.

Save each part

Each part keeps its answers when you press the Save button at its foot. While an answer is still missing, it takes you to the first part that misses one; when none is, on to the next part. Click Home, Search, Track or CV in the sidebar to see the Setting up screen again, with what is left.

Done whenthe three parts on Setting up have a tick. Only The job mailbox is left: that is the next step.

Before you start

Five things make setting up quick. Have them at hand, and the rest takes about ten minutes.

  1. A computer that is on during the day. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) now; the Mac version is on its way. GoodLuck! works while it is open, so a computer that sleeps all day finds nothing.
  2. Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Edge comes with Windows. GoodLuck! uses it to read job sites, in a window of its own that opens while it reads, and to print PDFs.
  3. Your CV, as a Word file (.docx) or a PDF. A PDF has to carry its text: a scan is pictures of pages, with nothing in it to read.
  4. The Gmail address you give employers, and its password. GoodLuck! follows your applications by reading this mailbox, so it has to be the address on your CV.
  5. Telegram on your phone: optional, and worth it. It is free on the App Store and Google Play, and it is how GoodLuck! reaches you when you are away from the computer.
Ready whenyou have your CV file, you can sign in to the Gmail account you apply with, and Telegram is on your phone.

Connect Gmail

GoodLuck! follows your applications by reading the mailbox employers reply to. It signs in with an app password: 16 letters Google makes for one app, which you can take back at any time. Your Google password never goes into GoodLuck!.

You needthe Gmail address on your CV (the Contact email you gave in step 3), and your phone, for Google's 2-Step Verification.

1. Turn on 2-Step Verification

Google makes app passwords only for accounts with 2-Step Verification. If yours has it already, go straight to part 2.

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/security and sign in with the Gmail address you apply with.
  2. Find 2-Step Verification and follow Google's steps to turn it on. It asks for your phone number and sends a code.

2. Make the app password

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Google may ask for your password again.
  2. Type a name for it, such as GoodLuck, and press Create.
  3. Google shows a password of 16 letters, in four groups. Copy it now, because Google shows it only once. Then press Done.

A drawing of Google's page, kept to what matters: the name you type, and the 16 letters it gives you.

3. Paste it into GoodLuck!

  1. On Setting up, find The job mailbox. Once your profile is complete, its form is already open; if it is not, press its Connect to open it. The address is filled in with your contact email: leave it as it is.
  2. Paste the 16 letters in the box 16-character app password. Spaces do not matter.
  3. Press the Connect under the boxes. It shows checking… while GoodLuck! signs in to your mailbox, to be sure it works before anything is saved.
Done whenthe page reloads and the card reads ✓ The job mailbox, with your address. If your profile was complete, the Setting up screen is gone for good: GoodLuck! is ready.

What GoodLuck! does with your mailbox

  • It only reads. It sends nothing, deletes nothing, moves nothing and marks nothing as read.
  • At each round it looks through the last 30 days of your inbox for the mail about your applications, and keeps what it needs on your computer.
  • The app password is kept in Windows Credential Manager, not in a file.
  • To take the password back, delete it at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Changing your Google password takes it back too. Then make a new one and paste it in Settings, Gmail, Change the app password.
  • Settings, Gmail, also chooses how far back to read, and Watch the mailbox reads it every 1, 5 or 15 minutes while a session is on, instead of waiting for the next round.
If it does not work
  • this mailbox (…) differs from the profile's address: connect the address you gave as Contact email, or change Contact email in Profile, Who you are.
  • that is not an app password (16 lowercase letters): paste the 16 letters Google showed, not your Google password.
  • Gmail refused.: the app password is wrong, or it was deleted. Make a new one.
  • Google has no App passwords page for you: 2-Step Verification is off, or the account belongs to a school or an employer that does not allow app passwords. Apply with a personal Gmail account.

The GoodLuck! guide

From the download to a routine of ten minutes a day, in seven steps. Follow them in order the first time: each one says what to press, what you should see when it is done, and what to do if you do not.

  1. Before you start: what to have at hand. Two minutes.
  2. Install: GoodLuck! on your computer. Two minutes.
  3. Your profile: your CV, the jobs you want, your skills. Five minutes.
  4. Connect Gmail: so replies update your applications by themselves. Three minutes.
  5. Connect your phone: Telegram, to hear from the app wherever you are. Three minutes.
  6. Your first session: start it, and watch the first round.
  7. Ten minutes a day: the routine, and what to do with each job.
Try it as you readEvery screen in this guide is the real app, running in your browser on fictional data, with the place to press ringed. Open the whole demo.

Look it up

Every screen explains each tab, panel and button of the app. When something is wrong lists the messages you might meet, and what to do about each one.

Your first session

A session is GoodLuck! at work: a round straight away, then one every hour, for as long as the app is open. Starting one is a single button.

  1. On Home, press Start session on the bar at the top.
  2. A round begins. First Search: job sites, companies' job boards and the job alerts in your mailbox, then filtering and scoring what is new. Then Manage mail: your mailbox, and the applications it moves.
  3. While it runs, the bar lights up and the journal along the bottom says what it is doing, line by line.
The first round takes the longestIt reads every job site and board from the start, and opens each posting it keeps to score it: allow up to half an hour. Later rounds read only what is new. Leave it running and come back later.
Done whenthe journal says the session is done, with 2/2 stages ran; the button now reads Run a round now; and the line at the very bottom says on watch · next round in 60 min. The new jobs are on Home, under Best score.

While it runs

  • The key. While a session is on, the GoodLuck! key sits by the clock; it may be under the ^ arrow. Click it to bring the window back. Right-click it for its menu: a line saying what the app is doing, then Open GoodLuck!, Run a round now, End session and Quit GoodLuck!.
  • Close the window, and the session carries on behind it. With no session on, closing the window quits the app.
  • Run a round now runs one at once. Stop ends the session: nothing runs by itself until you start one again.
  • Job sites are read in the daytime only: 8:00 to 22:00, unless you change it in Settings, Run. Company boards and your mail are read at every round.
  • Every hour is the default. Settings, Run, Rescan every takes 5 to 1440 minutes.
  • A session at every start: Settings, General, When the app opens, choose start a session.

What a session does

The ⚟ button on the bar, drawn as three sliders, opens what a session contains: Search and Manage mail, each on or off, and the sources a search reads, each on or off. Leave them all on unless you have a reason.

If it does not workThe journal says what went wrong, in a line marked ⚠. The usual ones are in When something is wrong.

Download GoodLuck!

Free for your own job search. No account, no subscription, nothing to sign up for.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
Download for Windows
  • Run it and press Next, Next, Install. It is installed for you alone: no administrator needed.
  • If Windows says it protected your PC, press More info, then Run anyway.
  • The duck by the clock says it is running. Watch it, step by step.

Mac

macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon
Download for Mac
  • Open it and drag GoodLuck onto Applications.
  • The first time, macOS says it cannot check GoodLuck. Close the message, then in System Settings, Privacy & Security, press Open Anyway.
  • For a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), not one with an Intel processor.

Before you download

GoodLuck! is free to use for your own job search. By downloading it you accept the license. In short:

  • You may install it on your own computers and use it for your own search, for as long as you like.
  • You may not sell it, use it in or for a business, pass copies on (point people here instead), or take it apart.

Then

Setting it up takes about ten minutes, in its own chat: your CV, the mailbox you give employers, your target. The tour shows every step on a real Windows screen.

A new version

Download it here. On Windows, run the new installer; on a Mac, drag it onto Applications and replace the old one. Your search, your applications and your settings are kept: they live in their own folder, not in the app.

Earlier versions and the notes for each one are on the releases page.

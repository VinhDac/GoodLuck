How to use it

Six steps, then it runs by itself.

About fifteen minutes to set up, then a few minutes a day. Each step shows the screen it happens on, with the place to click ringed.

Tell it what you want

Your job titles decide which jobs it keeps. Your CV and skills decide how each one scores.

  1. On the first screen, press Import your CV (.docx or PDF), then Save selected to keep what it read.
  2. In What you want, click the job titles under the box. The list follows each pick: aim for 15 to 20.
  3. Do the same for Your skills, answer the rest and press Save.
Full steps

Connect your Gmail

Replies from employers then update your applications by themselves. GoodLuck! only reads your mail.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google account.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type GoodLuck, press Create and copy the 16 letters.
  3. In GoodLuck!, under The job mailbox on the first screen, paste them and press Connect.
Full steps

Connect your phone

Telegram brings each search's new jobs and every reply to your phone, and lets you run GoodLuck! from it.

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, choose a name, then a username ending in bot. Copy the token it gives you.
  2. In GoodLuck!, open Settings, Notifications, paste the token and press Save.
  3. Open the link it shows, press Start, press Save again, then Test.
Full steps

Start a session

A session runs a round straight away, then one every hour: it searches, scores what is new, and reads your mail.

  1. Press Start session on the bar at the top of Home.
  2. The bar lights up while a round runs, and the journal says what it is doing. The first round takes the longest.
  3. Close the window if you like: the session carries on, with its key by the clock, or in the menu bar on a Mac.
Full steps

Every morning, apply to the best

Jobs that reach your mark, 70 unless you change it, wait on Home under Best score, newest first.

  1. On Home, open a job from Best score.
  2. Read Why this score: each thing the posting asks for, and the line of your CV that shows it.
  3. Apply on the employer's site, then press I applied. The job moves to Track.
Full steps

Let the replies come to you

Confirmations, interview invitations and rejections move each application along at every round.

  1. Open Track: every application, grouped by where it stands.
  2. When Queue shows a number, open it. Each mail the app could not place waits there for one click.
  3. An application silent for 20 days is treated as rejected, so Track shows what is still alive.

Private by design

Your data stays on your computer.

There is no GoodLuck! account and no GoodLuck! server. The app runs on your computer and keeps everything there.

Read the privacy note
  • Nothing sent to us. We never see your CV, your applications or your mail.
  • Passwords in the system's own vault: Keychain on a Mac, Credential Manager on Windows.
  • Your mailbox is only read. What the app reads there stays on your computer, and nothing is ever sent from it.
  • It never applies for you. Every application is yours to send.
Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. GoodLuck! is free for personal use, with no account and no subscription. It may not be used commercially or passed on to others; the license says exactly what is allowed.

Which computers does it run on?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 12 or later, and Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave; Edge comes with Windows. A Mac with an Intel processor cannot run it.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No. It finds, scores and keeps track. You decide where to apply, and you send every application yourself.

Where does it look for jobs?

On job sites, searched for your job titles in the countries you chose; on companies' own job boards (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby); and in the job alerts that arrive in your inbox. Job sites are read in a browser window of the app's own, without signing in.

What does a score mean?

The share of what a posting asks for that your profile and CV show, from 0 to 100. Your mark is 70 unless you change it in Settings. A job at or above it is a best score.

Can I move to another computer?

Yes. Settings exports everything into one file, and a new copy of GoodLuck! takes it back in, applications and all.

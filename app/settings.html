The SEARCH Chrome runs on a profile of its own, logged into no account, reading only public careers pages. On cookies it only presses Reject. Blocked, it stops and journals it rather than arguing. The app never types a password.
Export
Everything you have done here, in one file in Downloads: your profile, your CVs, these settings, the jobs found and their scores, Track and its mail, the words you struck off on the CV tab, and the mailbox and the phone, so an import connects them again. The file holds your Gmail app password and Telegram token: keep it to yourself.
Import
Carries on from a file Export made, or the one Start over leaves on the Desktop. It replaces everything here: what is here now is exported to Downloads first, so nothing is lost. The session stays off until you press Start session.
Wipes the profile, the postings scanned, the CVs built, the applications tracked, the mailbox connection and the Chrome profile: returning the app to the moment it was installed. Will be lost: 7,895 rows of data · 1 files · ~10.9 MB.
Before deleting, the app packs everything into a .tar.gz at ~/Desktop, the mailbox and phone passwords included, so importing it puts everything back. If that pack fails, NOTHING is deleted.

# GoodLuck!

**Your job search, every hour, on your own computer.**

GoodLuck! finds new jobs while you get on with your day, scores each one against your CV, and keeps your applications up to date from your inbox. It runs every hour, so your search never starts over.

Free for personal use, on Mac and Windows.

- **Website and live demo:** https://vinhdac.github.io/GoodLuck/
- **Download:** [GoodLuck! 1.3.0](https://vinhdac.github.io/GoodLuck/download/)
- **Guide:** https://vinhdac.github.io/GoodLuck/guide/
- **Report a problem:** [Issues](https://github.com/VinhDac/GoodLuck/issues)

## What it does

- **A search that keeps going.** Job sites, companies' own job boards and the job alerts in your inbox, read every hour and sorted for you.
- **A score you can read.** Every job is scored by how much of what it asks for your CV already shows, and you can see why.
- **Applications that track themselves.** Confirmations, interview invitations and rejections update your applications as they arrive.
- **What your CV is missing.** What the jobs you want keep asking for that your CV never mentions.

## Private by design

There is no GoodLuck! account and no GoodLuck! server. The app runs on your computer and keeps everything there. Read the [privacy note](https://vinhdac.github.io/GoodLuck/privacy/).
